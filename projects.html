PROJECT 03

Single Story to Double Story Convertion

Not for Sale COMPLETED
Land: 3.5 Cents
Area: 1450 Sq Ft

This residential renovation project successfully transformed a single-story home—originally featuring a car porch, sit-out, dining room, living hall, kitchen, work area, and 3 bedrooms with attached bathrooms—into a modern, spacious double-story structure designed for upgraded living. The redesigned ground floor now accommodates a car porch, sit-out, dedicated visiting room, dining room, kitchen, 2 functional work areas, and 2 bedrooms with attached bathrooms. An entirely new first floor was added, featuring a grand main hall, 1 balcony, and 2 additional bedrooms with attached bathrooms, significantly expanding the living space while delivering enhanced privacy, improved flow, and contemporary architectural appeal. Key upgrades included complete flooring overhaul, wall strengthening, modern kitchen remodeling, and premium exterior elevation painting and lighting.

PROJECT 02

Double Story Residential Building

SOLD OUT COMPLETED
Land: 3.5 Cents
Area: 1450 Sq Ft

This expertly crafted 1450 sq. ft. double-story residential building, located at Neerkode (Kallupalam), Aluva, is a fine example of modern space optimization built thoughtfully on a compact 3.5-cent plot. Designed for comfortable family living, the ground floor features a well-defined layout comprising a car porch, welcoming sit-out, dedicated visiting room, dining space, modern kitchen, and 1 bedroom with an attached bathroom. The first floor houses 2 spacious bedrooms—each with attached bathrooms—and seamlessly connects to a scenic balcony and a thoughtfully designed open terrace, ideal for outdoor relaxation. Combining smart spatial planning with modern aesthetics, this project highlights our commitment to delivering high-quality, functional homes tailored to urban land dimensions. Key upgrades included complete flooring overhaul, wall strengthening, modern kitchen remodeling, and premium exterior elevation painting and lighting.
